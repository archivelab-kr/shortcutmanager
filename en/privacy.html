Privacy policy

ShortcutManager keeps your setup on your Mac. This page explains what the app stores, what it sends, and how to turn sending off.

What stays on your Mac

What is on your pie, your shortcuts and your settings are saved on your Mac only, inside the app's own folder.

For a folder or file you put on the pie, the app keeps a bookmark macOS gives it so it can open that folder or file again. It does not read or copy other files.

There is no account and no sync. Nothing of the above is sent to us or to anyone else.

What is sent

To find and fix problems, the app sends anonymous crash reports and usage statistics through Google's Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics).

This includes: crash details, which features are used (for example that the pie was opened or a slice was changed, and the number of the slice), the app version, the macOS version, the Mac model, the language and the country, and a random ID for this installation.

It never includes: the names of your apps, folders or files, your shortcuts, or anything you type.

The data is not linked to your identity, is not used to track you across apps or websites, and is not sold or used for advertising.

Turning it off

Open Settings › General and switch off "Send anonymous crash reports and usage statistics". From then on the app sends nothing.

Purchases

The purchase that unlocks all slices is handled by Apple. We do not receive your name, your Apple Account or your payment details; the app only learns whether the purchase is active.

Permissions

The app does not ask for Accessibility, Input Monitoring, Screen Recording or any other permission. It notices the Command key being tapped twice through the modifier-key state macOS reports; it cannot see what you type.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
