Support

Write to archivelabapps@gmail.com and we'll help. Please include your macOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

The pie does not open

Tap the left Command key twice, quickly, without pressing anything else. Holding the key does not count. You can choose another key in Settings › Options. Check that ShortcutManager is running: its icon is in the menu bar.

How do I change what is on a slice?

Open Settings from the menu bar icon (or press comma while the pie is open). On the Pie tab, click a slice and choose an app, a folder or a file. "Leave it empty" clears it.

A shortcut is not accepted

A shortcut needs Command or Control; macOS does not give apps from the App Store shortcuts with only Option or Shift. If nothing happens when you press the keys, another app is already using that combination.

Some slices show a lock

Three slices (Q, W, E) and one shortcut are free. Choose a locked slice, or Settings › General › "Unlock everything…", to buy the unlock once.

I bought the unlock on another Mac

Open the unlock window and choose "Restore Purchase" while signed in to the same Apple Account.

It is not running after I restart my Mac

Switch on Settings › General › "Open at login".

A folder or file slice stopped working

The folder or file was moved or renamed. Click its slice in Settings and choose it again.

How do I hide the menu bar icon, and get back to Settings?

Switch off Settings › General › "Show in the menu bar". Open ShortcutManager again from Finder or Spotlight to show Settings.

How do I remove the app?

Quit ShortcutManager from its menu bar icon and move it from Applications to the Trash.
